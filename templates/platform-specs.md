# Platform Specifications & Best Practices

## Technical Requirements by Platform

### Google Business Profile (GBP)

**Character Limits:**
- Post text: 1,500 characters maximum
- Optimal: 300-750 characters (users see first 100-150 before "more")

**Media:**
- Images: JPG, PNG
- Size: 250KB - 5MB
- Dimensions: 720x540 (4:3) or 720x720 (1:1) recommended
- Video: 30 seconds max, 100MB max

**Best Practices:**
- Lead with value in first 100 characters
- Include location keywords (North Canton, Canton)
- Add clear CTA with phone number
- Post 2-4 times per week
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours

**Optimal Post Times:**
- Monday-Friday: 8:00 AM (morning search peak)
- Saturday: 9:00 AM
- Avoid: Sundays (low engagement)

**Content That Performs:**
- Service updates
- Seasonal reminders
- Local project showcases
- Pricing transparency
- Educational tips

**Red Flags:**
- ❌ Hashtags (not supported)
- ❌ Emojis (use sparingly if at all)
- ❌ Multiple CTAs (one clear action)
- ❌ Generic content (be local-specific)

### Facebook

**Character Limits:**
- Post text: No hard limit
- Optimal: 40-80 characters (most engagement)
- Up to 400 words acceptable for storytelling

**Media:**
- Images: JPG, PNG, GIF
- Size: Up to 4MB recommended
- Dimensions: 1200x630 (link posts), 1080x1080 (feed posts)
- Video: Up to 240 minutes, 4GB max
- Multiple images: Up to 10 per post

**Best Practices:**
- Start with a question or hook
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
- Use line breaks for readability
- Include visual content always
- Encourage comments (algorithm boost)
- Tag location (North Canton, Ohio)

**Optimal Post Times:**
- Monday-Friday: 1:00-3:00 PM
- Saturday-Sunday: 12:00-1:00 PM
- Avoid: Early mornings (low engagement)

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- Use 0-2 hashtags maximum
- Facebook algorithm doesn't prioritize hashtags
- Only use: #NorthCanton #CantonOhio

**Content That Performs:**
- Before/after photos
- Behind-the-scenes
- Customer stories (with permission)
- Educational tips
- Community engagement questions

**Post Frequency:**
- 3-5 times per week ideal
- Consistency > frequency
- Mix organic and occasional promotion

---

### LinkedIn

**Character Limits:**
- Post text: 3,000 characters max
- Optimal: 1,300-2,000 characters (engagement sweet spot)
- First 140 characters show before "see more"

**Media:**
- Images: JPG, PNG, GIF
- Size: 5MB max for images
- Dimensions: 1200x627 (link), 1200x1200 (single), 1104x736 (carousel)
- Video: 10 minutes max (but 30 seconds ideal), 5GB max
- Documents: PDF, PPT, DOC (up to 300MB)

**Best Practices:**
- Lead with business insight or lesson
- Professional but conversational tone
- Include metrics or specific examples
- Use 5-8 hashtags (LinkedIn algorithm uses them)
- Tag relevant connections when appropriate
- Position as peer, not salesperson

**Optimal Post Times:**
- Tuesday-Thursday: 7:30 AM or 5:00-6:00 PM
- Monday/Friday: 8:00 AM
- Avoid: Weekends (87% less engagement)

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- 5-8 hashtags optimal
- Mix of broad and specific
- Examples: #SmallBusiness #Landscaping #PropertyMaintenance #Canton #CustomerService

**Content That Performs:**
- Business lessons learned
- Industry insights
- Professional challenges/solutions
- B2B relationship content
- Thought leadership

**Post Frequency:**
- 2-3 times per week
- Quality > quantity
- Engage with others' content daily

### Instagram

**Character Limits:**
- Caption: 2,200 characters max
- Optimal: First 125 characters critical (before "more")
- Hashtags: 30 max (but 12-15 recommended)

**Media Requirements:**

**Feed Posts:**
- Images: JPG, PNG
- Size: Up to 30MB (8MB recommended)
- Dimensions:
  - Square: 1080x1080
  - Portrait: 1080x1350 (4:5 ratio)
  - Landscape: 1080x566
- Carousel: Up to 10 images/videos
- Video: 60 seconds max in feed

**Stories:**
- Dimensions: 1080x1920 (9:16 ratio)
- Duration: 15 seconds per story segment
- Format: JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV

**Reels:**
- Duration: 15-90 seconds
- Dimensions: 1080x1920 (9:16 ratio)
- Format: MP4, MOV
- Size: Up to 4GB

**Best Practices:**
- Hook in first line (before "more" cutoff)
- Use line breaks for readability
- Tell stories, not just promote
- Location tags boost local visibility
- Save-worthy content gets algorithm boost
- Respond to comments quickly

**Optimal Post Times:**
- Daily: 11:00 AM or 7:00-9:00 PM
- Saturday-Sunday: 10:00 AM-2:00 PM
- Avoid: Late night/early morning

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- 12-15 hashtags optimal (research shows)
- Mix of sizes:
  - 3-5 popular (#HomeImprovement) 100K-1M posts
  - 5-7 medium (#NorthCantonOhio) 10K-100K posts
  - 3-5 niche (#NorthCantonLandscaping) <10K posts
- Place at end of caption or first comment
- Rotate hashtag sets (don't reuse exact same 15)

**Example Hashtag Set:**
```
#RetainingWall #Hardscape #LandscapingCosts
#NorthCanton #CantonOhio #StarkCounty
#PropertyMaintenance #LandscapingTips
#HomeImprovement #YardWork #OutdoorLiving
#DIYvsHire #Landscaper #NortheastOhio #OhioLandscaping
```

**Content That Performs:**
- Carousel posts (swipe-through)
- Before/after reveals
- Educational content (save-worthy)
- Behind-the-scenes
- Time-lapse videos
- Infographic-style tips

**Post Frequency:**
- Feed: 3-5 times per week
- Stories: Daily (if you have content)
- Reels: 2-3 per week (algorithm favors)

### Email Newsletter

**Subject Line:**
- 50 characters or less ideal (mobile display)
- Avoid spam triggers (FREE, !!!, BUY NOW)
- Use name personalization: "Hi Jordan,"
- Test: Question vs statement

**Content Length:**
- Email body: 200-500 words optimal
- Longer okay for valuable content (up to 1,000)
- Use sections/headers for scannability

**Format:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullet points for lists
- Clear hierarchy (headers, subheads)
- One primary CTA
- Mobile-responsive always

**Best Practices:**
- Personal from name (Jordan, not Fili Property Maintenance)
- From email: [email protected] (not noreply@)
- Preheader text: First 35-90 characters after subject
- Alt text for images (many block images)
- Plain text version available

**Optimal Send Times:**
- Tuesday-Thursday: 10:00 AM
- Avoid: Mondays (inbox overload), Fridays (check-out mode)

**Frequency:**
- Weekly: Too much for most small businesses
- Bi-weekly: Good for consistent value
- Monthly: Acceptable but less engagement

**Content Mix:**
- 80% value/education, 20% promotion
- Seasonal tips
- Project showcases
- Behind-the-scenes
- Special offers (occasional)

**Email Metrics:**
- Open rate: 20-30% good for small business
- Click rate: 2-5% is solid
- Unsubscribe: <1% healthy

## Cross-Platform Strategy

### Content Repurposing Flow

**Blog Post (Master Content)**
↓
**Email Newsletter** (excerpt with link to full post)
↓
**LinkedIn** (professional angle, business lesson)
↓
**Facebook** (storytelling version, engagement question)
↓
**Instagram** (visual + tips, carousel format)
↓
**GBP** (local angle, CTA to call)

### Posting Cadence Example Week

**Monday:**
- GBP: 8:00 AM (local service reminder)

**Tuesday:**
- Email: 10:00 AM (newsletter with blog link)
- LinkedIn: 7:30 AM (professional insight)

**Wednesday:**
- Facebook: 1:00 PM (story-based post)

**Thursday:**
- Instagram: 11:00 AM (visual tip)
- LinkedIn: 5:00 PM (if B2B content)

**Friday:**
- Facebook: 1:00 PM (weekend prep reminder)

**Saturday:**
- Instagram: 10:00 AM (weekend content)

**Sunday:**
- Rest (low engagement across all platforms)

### Content Themes by Day

- **Monday:** Motivational/new week energy
- **Tuesday:** Educational/how-to
- **Wednesday:** Behind-the-scenes
- **Thursday:** Tips/quick wins
- **Friday:** Weekend projects/inspiration
- **Saturday:** Visual showcases
- **Sunday:** Off (or light community content)

## Engagement Best Practices

### Response Times
- GBP reviews: Within 24 hours
- Social comments: Within 2-4 hours during business hours
- Direct messages: Within 1-2 hours
- Email: Within 24 hours

### What to Respond To
✅ All questions
✅ All complaints (publicly and positively)
✅ Positive feedback (thank them!)
✅ Tags/mentions
✅ Engagement on your prompts

### What to Ignore
❌ Spam
❌ Trolls (delete if offensive)
❌ Irrelevant tagging

### Response Tone
- Appreciative for positive
- Helpful for questions
- Professional but empathetic for concerns
- Quick and clear always
