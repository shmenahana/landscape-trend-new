## Platform-Specific Adaptations

### Google Business Profile (GBP)
**Character Limit:** 1,500 characters
**Tone:** Professional but approachable, local-focused
**Purpose:** Appear in local search, drive calls/texts
**Requirements:**
- Lead with value/benefit
- Include location (North Canton, Canton, Louisville)
- Add phone number or "Call/Text today"
- Use local neighborhood names
- Include service keywords
- End with clear CTA

**Structure:**
```
[Hook - 1 sentence about the problem/season]

[2-3 sentences about the solution/service]

[Local context - mention neighborhoods or specific examples]

[Proof point - years in business, reviews, current clients]

[CTA - "Call 330-XXX-XXXX for your free estimate in North Canton"]
```

**Example:**
"September's here, which means it's prime aeration time in North Canton. Our clay-heavy soil needs those holes punched in fall—not spring—to let roots breathe before winter.

I've been doing this for 15 years around the Canton area, and I can tell you: the lawns that get fall aeration always come back thicker in May. Working with 47 residential clients this month across Price Street, Wise Ave, and the Dogwood Park area.

Free estimates for North Canton and Louisville. Call 330-XXX-XXXX or text me today. 89 five-star reviews on Google."

### Facebook
**Character Limit:** No hard limit, but 200-300 words optimal
**Tone:** Conversational, friendly, story-driven
**Purpose:** Community engagement, trust building
**Requirements:**
- Start with a question or relatable problem
- Tell a brief story or give an example
- Include a photo reference (even if no photo attached)
- Conversational language
- Engagement bait (ask a question at the end)
- CTA but softer than GBP

**Structure:**
```
[Question or relatable opening]

[Short story or example from a recent job]

[Educational tip or insight]

[Soft CTA or question for engagement]
```

**Example:**
"Anyone else noticing their lawn looking a little thin after this hot summer?

Just finished aerating a property over on Deerfield Ave yesterday—homeowner said it's been getting worse every year. Turns out they'd been aerating in spring (like everyone thinks you should), but that's actually when crabgrass seeds are trying to germinate. Fall aeration in our clay soil? Game changer.

Quick tip: If you can't stick a screwdriver 6 inches into your soil easily, you need aeration. Our North Canton clay gets compacted FAST.

Anyone already aerate this fall? Drop a comment below 👇"

### LinkedIn
**Character Limit:** 1,300-2,000 characters optimal
**Tone:** Professional, business-owner-to-business-owner
**Purpose:** Build authority, generate B2B referrals
**Requirements:**
- Lead with business insight or lesson
- Professional but still authentic
- Include metrics or specific examples
- Position as peer/expert, not salesperson
- Tag industry-relevant topics
- CTA for professional connection

**Structure:**
```
[Business insight or lesson learned]

[Detailed example from your work]

[Advice or takeaway for other business owners]

[Professional CTA]
```

**Example:**
"15 years in the landscaping business taught me: pricing transparency builds more trust than any marketing could.

Last week I gave a customer an 18-page packet breaking down why their failing 8.5-foot retaining wall would cost $45K-75K to replace. I also told them it was beyond my capabilities and gave them 3 structural engineers to call.

You know what happened? They referred me to two of their neighbors for smaller projects, and one of those engineers called me to partner on residential work.

Being honest about what you CAN'T do earns more respect than overselling what you can.

If you're in the Canton/Akron area and need property maintenance or hardscaping, let's connect. Always looking for commercial property managers to work with.

#SmallBusiness #Landscaping #CustomerService #Canton"

### Instagram Caption
**Character Limit:** 2,200 characters but first 125 characters matter most
**Tone:** Visual-first, casual, engaging
**Purpose:** Brand awareness, visual portfolio
**Requirements:**
- Hook in first line (before "more" cut-off)
- Emoji usage (but not excessive)
- Line breaks for readability
- 8-15 hashtags
- Story-driven
- Tag location
- Multiple CTAs (like, save, DM)

**Structure:**
```
[Hook - single compelling line]

[2-3 line breaks for spacing]

[Short story or tip]

[More breaks]

[CTA]

[Hashtag block]
```

**Example:**
"Your retaining wall is rotting. Here's what it'll actually cost to replace it. 💰

Those railroad tie walls from the 90s? Yeah, they're all failing now. Just quoted two on Deerfield Ave this month—same problem, same solution needed.

Here's the real numbers for North Canton:
• Segmental block wall: $45K-75K (8.5 ft tall)
• Soldier pile system: $60K-95K
• Concrete cantilever: $65K-100K

Not trying to scare you. Just being real about costs so you can plan. Most contractors won't tell you this upfront.

Got questions about your wall? DM me. Free estimates in Canton/North Canton area.

📍 North Canton, Ohio

#RetainingWall #Hardscape #LandscapingCosts #NorthCanton #CantonOhio #PropertyMaintenance #LandscapingTips #HomeImprovement #YardWork #OutdoorLiving #DIYvsHire #HomeRenovation #Landscaper #StarkCounty #NortheastOhio"

### Email Newsletter Snippet
**Length:** 150-250 words per section
**Tone:** Informative, helpful, builds relationship
**Purpose:** Stay top-of-mind, provide value
**Requirements:**
- Subject line hook
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullet points for scannability
- Seasonal relevance
- Value before pitch
- Clear "learn more" link

**Structure:**
```
Subject: [Seasonal hook + value prop]

Hi [First Name],

[Quick seasonal insight]

[Educational content in bullets or short paragraphs]

[Soft CTA to website or call]

[Signature]
```

**Purpose:** Rank in search, establish authority

- Local specificity throughout
