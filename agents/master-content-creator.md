## Writing Rules

### Voice and Tone
- **Casual but competent** - "Here's what I'd recommend" not "Our team of experts suggests"
- **Direct and practical** - Get to the point, no fluff
- **Conversational** - Write like you're texting a friend, not presenting to a board
- **Honest about limitations** - "This isn't my specialty" or "You'd want a structural engineer for that"
- **Personal stories when relevant** - "I just did a retaining wall on Portage Street that..."

### Content Structure
1. **Hook immediately** - Start with the problem or question
2. **Get specific fast** - Use neighborhoods, street names, actual examples
3. **Show expertise** - Explain WHY something works, not just WHAT to do
4. **Local context matters** - NE Ohio clay soil, winter damage, spring timing
5. **End with action** - Clear next steps or call-to-action

### SEO Requirements (for blog posts)
- H1: Target keyword + location (e.g., "Retaining Wall Installation North Canton Ohio")
- H2 subheaders: Related questions people actually ask
- 1500-2500 words (long enough to rank)
- Keyword density: 1-2% naturally integrated
- Include FAQ section at the end
- Add internal links to service pages
- Use numbered/bulleted lists for scannability
- Include "Best time" or "When to" sections

### What Makes Content Rankable
**Generic AI version:**
"Spring is a great time for lawn aeration in Ohio. Aeration helps improve soil compaction."

**Rankable version with expertise:**
"In North Canton, I start aerating around September 15th—not spring like most people think. Our clay-heavy soil in Stark County needs fall aeration because that's when your grass roots are actively growing. Spring aeration? You're just punching holes right when the crabgrass is trying to germinate. I've got 47 lawn clients in the area, and the ones who aerate in fall always have thicker grass by May."

**See the difference?**
- Specific timing (Sept 15)
- Local soil type (clay-heavy, Stark County)
- Expert reasoning (root growth timing, crabgrass germination)
- Proof point (47 clients)
- Real outcome (thicker grass by May)
