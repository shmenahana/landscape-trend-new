## Writing Rules

### Voice and Tone (CRITICAL)
- **Educational and helpful** - "Understanding typical costs helps you budget appropriately" NOT "You'll regret not booking now!"
- **Professional expertise** - "Professional landscape design ensures cohesive results and prevents costly mistakes"
- **Comprehensive guides** - Thorough information that actually answers questions
- **Transparent and honest** - Clear about costs, process, and what to expect
- **Local specificity** - North Canton soil conditions, Ohio climate, regional timing

### What This Tone IS:
✅ "Timing your landscaping project correctly can mean the difference between a thriving outdoor space and a struggling landscape"
✅ "Understanding typical costs helps you budget appropriately and avoid surprises"
✅ "Professional landscape design ensures cohesive results and prevents costly mistakes"
✅ "Rather than rushing projects during marginal conditions, patient timing aligned with North Canton's climate patterns ensures better results"

### What This Tone is NOT:
❌ "By mid-March you'll be kicking yourself for not booking"
❌ "You're running out of time - slots filling fast!"
❌ "Book now before it's too late!"
❌ "Limited availability - act today!"
❌ Any pressure tactics or false urgency

1. **Clear, informative opening** - State the topic and why it matters

3. **Demonstrate expertise** - Explain WHY something works, not just WHAT to do

## Pricing Guidelines

### When to Include Pricing:

**YES - Include with context:**
- "Landscape Design: $500-$3,000 depending on property size and complexity"
- "Paver Patios: $15-$30 per square foot"
- "Basic Planting & Beds: $1,500-$5,000"
- "Retaining Walls: $25-$50 per square foot depending on height, materials, and site conditions"

**NO - Too variable or don't have data:**
- Spring cleanup (varies too much by property)
- Mulch installation (pricing model is time + materials, not simple per-yard)
- Custom projects without standard scope
- Services with too many variables

**Always include qualifiers:**
- "depending on property size and project scope"
- "based on materials and complexity"
- "varies based on specific conditions"
- "for typical North Canton properties"

### How to Handle Pricing from Approved Posts:

Look at this example structure:
```
Paver patios represent one of the most popular North Canton landscaping investments. Total costs typically range from $15-$30 per square foot installed, depending on materials and complexity.

For a typical North Canton patio (300-500 square feet):
- Basic concrete pavers: $4,500-$7,500
- Mid-range pavers: $6,000-$12,000
- Premium natural stone: $9,000-$15,000+

These prices include proper base preparation (critical in North Canton's clay soils), paver installation, edge restraint, and polymeric sand joints.
```

Notice:
- Range given ($15-$30/sq ft)
- Qualifier added ("depending on materials and complexity")
- Example scenarios with different price points
- Explains what's included in the price
- Local context (North Canton clay soils)
