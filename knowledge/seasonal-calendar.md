# Seasonal Calendar - North Canton, Ohio Landscaping

## Overview

This calendar provides optimal timing for landscaping services in North Canton, Ohio (USDA Zone 6a). Timing is critical due to clay soil, variable spring weather, and specific grass growth patterns.

## Monthly Breakdown

### JANUARY

**Weather:** Cold (20-35°F), snow cover typical, ground frozen
**Lawn Status:** Dormant
**What to Do:** Planning only

**Content Topics:**
- Plan spring projects now
- Equipment maintenance
- Design consultations
- Snow plowing (active service)

**Not Recommended:**
- Any outdoor work
- Planting
- Hardscaping

---

### FEBRUARY

**Weather:** Still cold, occasional thaw, unreliable
**Lawn Status:** Dormant
**What to Do:** Continue planning

**Content Topics:**
- Early spring planning
- "Book your spring cleanup now"
- Landscape design consultations
- Retaining wall project quotes

**Not Recommended:**
- Anything requiring ground work
- Planting

---

### MARCH

**Weather:** Variable (30-55°F), mud season, unpredictable
**Lawn Status:** Starting to wake up
**What to Do:** Wait for ground to firm up

**Spring Cleanup:** Late March only if ground is firm and dry

**Content Topics:**
- "When to start spring cleanup in North Canton"
- Timing is everything (why you can't rush it)
- Planning hardscape projects
- Mulch pre-orders

**Warning:**
Don't start too early - clay soil needs to dry and firm. Walking on wet clay causes compaction.

---

### APRIL

**Weather:** 40-65°F, rain common, last frost mid-month
**Lawn Status:** Active growth begins
**What to Do:** Spring cleanup, early planting, hardscape starts

**Prime Services:**

- ✅ Mulch installation
- ✅ Early season mowing starts
- ✅ Landscape bed preparation
- ✅ Hardscape projects begin
- ⚠️ Planting (wait until after April 15 for tender plants)

**Content Topics:**
- "Spring cleanup checklist North Canton"
- "When to plant in Zone 6a"
- "Hardscape season is here"
- Weekly mowing signup

**Timing Notes:**
- First mow: Usually last week of April
- Watch for late frosts (can happen until April 20)

**Lawn Status:** Peak spring growth
**What to Do:** Everything landscape-related

- ✅ Mulching
- ✅ Bed renovation

**Lawn Status:** Still growing actively, starting to slow in heat
**What to Do:** Maintenance mode, strategic planting

**Prime Services:**
- ✅ Weekly mowing (essential)
- ✅ Hardscape projects

**Content Topics:**

Not the best month for new plantings unless you're committed to watering. Better to wait until September.

- Fall/winter establishment before spring

## Critical Timing Points

### THE MOST IMPORTANT DATE: September 15

**Why This Date Matters:**
- Optimal aeration timing for North Canton clay soil
- Ground temperature perfect for seed germination
- Grass roots actively growing
- No crabgrass competition
- Results visible by following spring

**Content Strategy:**
Start pushing "September 15" in July and August. Make it THE date everyone knows.

**May 1:** Full-on spring planting season starts
**September 1:** Start pre-booking fall aeration
**September 15-October 31:** Core aeration window
**October 15:** Fall cleanup season peaks
**November 15:** Last mowing typically complete

## Weather Contingencies

### Early Spring / Late Fall
If ground is too wet or too frozen:
- Delay work (don't force it)
- Communicate to customers WHY
- Content: "Why We Wait: Protecting Your Clay Soil"

### Summer Drought
If extended hot/dry period:
- Reduce mowing frequency
- Content: "Drought Management in Clay Soil"
- Shift focus to fall booking

### Late Spring Frost
If frost threatens new plantings:
- Content: "Protecting Your Plants from Late Frost"
- Timing reminder for future years

---

**For Content Creation:** Every piece should reflect actual North Canton timing. Don't use generic "spring is a great time" - use "April 15 in North Canton" or "September 15 in clay soil." This specificity is what makes content rank and what makes customers trust you know the local conditions.
